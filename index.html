One look · Like or Nope

What you can do

FactSurveyGlobal is a simple visual preference survey. Brands need a quick read on which looks feel right. Like and Nope are preference taps only. There is no right or wrong answer.

View one photo at a time

Each screen shows a single look from the app photo set.

Tap Like or Nope

Tell us whether the look works, then move to the next photo.

No account needed

Use the app without creating an account. Progress stays on the device.

How it works

FactSurveyGlobal loads a photo set and stores your progress on the device. Photos are part of the app content. The app does not read your camera or gallery.

1

Open the app

A photo set loads. You do not upload personal photos.

2

Tap a preference

Like or Nope. The choice is saved on the phone.

3

Continue the set

Move through looks at your own pace.

Ads

The app may show rewarded video and interstitial ads in later sessions. You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings. FactSurveyGlobal is built for visual feedback, not for banking.

Privacy

No sign-up. No contacts, SMS, location or activity permission. Survey images are provided by the app.

  • Like / Nope history is stored on the device
  • Clear app data or uninstall to remove local progress
