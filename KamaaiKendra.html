What is Kamaai Kendra?+

Kamaai Kendra is an app where you can discover promotion campaigns, share links, and build rewards by helping your audience complete campaign tasks.

How do I start promoting?+

Download the APK, register inside the app, choose a campaign, and share your promotion link with your audience.

What kind of tasks can a campaign have?+

Every campaign can have different requirements, such as signing up, installing and activating, or completing a first action. Always check the campaign details.
