FactSurveyGlobal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026 · Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains how the FactSurveyGlobal mobile application (“App”, “we”, “us”) handles information. FactSurveyGlobal is a visual preference survey: you see one photo at a time and tap Like or Nope. The App does not create user accounts.

1. Who we are

FactSurveyGlobal is operated by the developer that publishes the App on Google Play. For privacy questions, email privacy@chalofit.club.

2. Information we process

We keep data collection to what the App needs to work:

  • Survey choices and local app state. Like / Nope taps, swipe count, local points, settings and similar values are stored on your device. We do not upload a survey history or a user profile to our servers.
  • Survey images. Photos you rate are part of the App’s photo set (bundled or loaded from our content host). The App does not read your camera, gallery or personal photos.
  • Remote configuration and content. The App may fetch feature flags and photo-set indexes over HTTPS. These requests are not tied to a login.
  • Advertising ID and device data for ads. If ads are shown, the advertising ID plus basic device and app data (OS version, device type, country, language, IP address processed by ad partners) may be used by our mediation SDK and ad networks to request, serve and measure ads.
  • Install / campaign data. Play Install Referrer and attribution partners may receive install source, campaign identifiers, and device/app technical data to measure ads and app opens.
  • App interactions for ads and analytics. Swipes and ad events (impressions, completions) may be processed by advertising and measurement partners.
  • Diagnostics. Crash or performance logs may be collected in anonymized or aggregated form if a crash tool is enabled.
  • Support messages. If you open a support or contact link (for example Telegram), that service processes whatever you send. We do not require a name or email to use the App.
  • Installed-app check for support links. The App may check whether Telegram is installed so it can open the matching support link. It does not read your full app list for any other purpose.

We do not ask for contacts, SMS, precise location, camera, microphone, physical activity, or payment card details. The App has no sign-up and no password.

3. How we use information

  • Show survey photos and store your taps on the device
  • Load remote configuration and photo-set indexes
  • Operate optional local points and optional in-app request screens
  • Show rewarded and interstitial ads when those features are on
  • Measure installs, opens and ad events
  • Open the correct support link if you tap contact or promotional support creatives
  • Comply with law and store policies

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your Like / Nope taps to build an advertising profile beyond what the ad and measurement SDKs do as described here.

4. Legal bases (if GDPR / similar laws apply)

Where those laws apply, we process data to provide the App you asked for, with your permission for ads where required, and for legitimate interests in measuring campaigns and keeping the App secure. You may reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings.

5. Sharing

Device-side survey taps and local points stay on the device unless you screenshot or copy them yourself. We share limited technical and advertising data with:

  • TopOn (ad mediation). See TopOn Privacy Policy.
  • Pangle. See Pangle Privacy Policy.
  • Unity Ads. See Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Liftoff (Vungle). See Vungle Privacy Policy.
  • InMobi. See InMobi Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play services. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Adjust (attribution, if enabled in the build). See Adjust Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram — only if you open a support link and send a message.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the App and users.

6. Retention

Local survey progress and points remain on the device until you clear app data or uninstall the App. Advertising and measurement partners retain data under their own policies. We do not operate a cloud profile for you.

7. Security

We store App state on-device using standard Android storage. Network calls for configuration, photos and ads use HTTPS. No method of storage is 100% secure. Keep your device locked and updated.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International users

The App is intended for users in India. Advertising and measurement partners may process data in other countries.

10. Your choices

  • Reset or delete advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Opt out of ads personalization where Google provides that control.
  • Clear local data or uninstall the App.

Because there is no account, we cannot remotely download a server copy of your survey taps. We do not keep one.

11. Google Play Data safety summary

  • Device or other IDs: advertising ID for ads and attribution
  • App activity: swipes and ad events for advertising and analytics
  • Diagnostics: crash logs if a crash SDK is enabled
  • Photos and videos: not collected from you; survey images are App content
  • Contacts, SMS, location, financial and health data: not collected
  • Survey taps stay on device and are not shared by us as a user profile

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

Privacy requests: privacy@chalofit.club
